# truck.blend — full-render review r2 (stills r6/r7 @ 1920x1080)

Stills: assets-src/blender/stills/truck/r6_*_full.png (f1–f216), r7_f108/150/175/194/216_full.png.

**First impression.** The aerial now reads as real coastal Andhra farmland: rectangular plots in rotated holdings, some with furrows, scrub zones, coconut orchards in rows, mango clumps, ponds in farm blocks with earthen bunds, a casuarina belt, the beach and the sea. The yard at f90/f108 is busy and worn. The camera climb is smoother, and a shorter shutter on the climb keeps it readable (f194).

Four questions
- Empty? No.
- Toy-like? Mango crowns close to the climb read as blobs at f175 (r6). r7 puts only coconut groves within ~250 m of the climb and has fuller, lower, rougher crowns elsewhere. Trees directly under the low climb are removed: a blurred foreground palm smudged f150.
- Fake? Pond bunds looked like grey concrete at f175 → final round uses a darker, browner earthen bund. The sea sheen is a broad glitter on swell, not a pillar.
- Clipping? Pond blocks are slid clear of the yard and the road, and blocks never overlap (f150, f175 clean).
